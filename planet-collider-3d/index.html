Speed is a multiple of the speed needed to escape each other. Tilt leans the second galaxy’s disc. Black holes, their discs and jets are drawn hugely enlarged; when two merge, about 5% of their mass (for an equal pair) leaves as gravitational waves. Stars orbit in the gravity of both galaxies. Invisible dark matter holds about 90% of the mass, and its drag is what makes galaxies merge. Values are rounded estimates.

Render a video

Renders the collision from launch as a smooth 1080p, 60 fps video (wide, or tall for Shorts, Reels and TikTok), however fast your computer is. Uses the current settings, view and playback speed. Higher particle counts are for videos only: they’re far too slow to watch live.
