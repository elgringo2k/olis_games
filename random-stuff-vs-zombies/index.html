Level complete!

Tap the glowing orbs to collect energy before they fade. Pick a card, then tap a tile to place it. Sun Squids make energy orbs you tap to collect. Battery Towers fire when you tap them: pay 50 energy to blast the zombie closest to your house for 500 damage, once every 5 seconds. Hypersquids are planted on top of a Sun Squid and make big 50-energy orbs, but fall asleep at night too. Mini Turtles are tiny, free night turtles that throw rocks up to 3 tiles ahead. Vampire Squids are cheaper and start small, making 10-energy orbs for their first 90 seconds before growing up and making the normal 25. Rock Turtles throw rocks down the whole lane. Snapper is a Venus flytrap that stays shut until a zombie gets within 1.25 tiles, opens up, swallows it whole once it's 1 tile away (anything except the Mutant), chews for 10 seconds, then burps. Chog-chog is a tiny hedgehog that only hurts zombies by being eaten: it explodes for 400 in a 3×3, or 1000 if it has grown for 13 seconds, or a humongous 1800 in a 5×5 if it has grown for 26 seconds. The Digger is an excavator that starts with a boulder in its bucket: tap it, then tap anywhere on the lawn to hurl the boulder for 500 damage, plus 250 to everything in the 3×3 around it. It digs up a new boulder 15 seconds later. Rusty the Dragon has two gears you switch by tapping him: in gear 1 he breathes fire up to 3 tiles ahead, and in gear 2 he falls asleep and any rock flying over him turns to lava and does double damage. Multurtles throw a rock down their own lane and the lanes above and below. Angry Turtles throw rocks two at a time, and an Enraged Turtle (planted on top of an Angry Turtle) throws four. Whip Turtles hit harder and faster, but only reach 3 tiles ahead. Mau Mau is a big brick that just sits there and soaks up bites. Forti Mau upgrades a Mau Mau you've already placed into a 10000-health fortress. Benny Bee flies out to sting the closest zombie in his lane and poisons it. Cleaning Spray mists every zombie in a 3-lane-wide, 2-tile-long patch in front of it. Shampoo leaves slippery pink puddles that slow zombies down. Laser Turtle fires a beam that hits every zombie in its lane. Lewis Lobster pinches hard but only reaches one tile ahead, so put him right behind a Mau Mau. Sammy the shark bites zombies up to 2 tiles ahead and makes them bleed. Hyperbadge hides half buried and claws at any zombie walking over him. Jicjajic does nothing by itself, line up a Rock Turtle, a Whip Turtle and a Laser Turtle in a row (across a lane or up and down) and plant it on the middle one and it merges all three into a Hyper Turtle seed packet. Tap the packet within 10 seconds to collect it, then plant the two-tile Hyper Turtle anywhere. Plant it on the middle of three Battery Towers in a row for a Tesla Coil: tap it and pay 100 energy for a 1500-damage bolt that also splashes 750 across the 3×3 around its target, every 10 seconds. Plant it on the middle of three Snappers in a row for an Ultima Snapper: a 2-tile flytrap with big teeth that gulps up to 5 zombies at once, even a Mutant. It can also fuse a Shampoo and a Cleaning Spray that sit next to each other: plant it on either one to get a Squeaky Clean seed packet. Squeaky Clean sprays the same 3-lane by 2-tile area as Cleaning Spray for 60 damage and leaves slippery shampoo on every zombie it hits. Temper-lotl loses his temper 1.5 seconds after you place him and explodes for 1800 damage in a 3×3 area. Use the shovel to dig up a defender you don't want. Watch for Shield Bearers from wave 2, Soup Can Heads from wave 3, Runnererers from wave 4, Pool Noodlers from wave 5, Charging Knights from wave 6, Teachers from wave 5 (break their test and they get furious), and a single Mutant in wave 8.
